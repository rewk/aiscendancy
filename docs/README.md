# docs

The findings, split by subject:

- `formats.md`: the game's files, tables, banks, measured rectangles.
- `screens.md`: the original's screens and the population-capacity data.
- `rules.md`: every rule with its source and status, the open assumptions.
- `galaxy.md`: the star cluster generator.

The rules themselves live in `game/`. Below, the external sources.

- `third-party/` (git-ignored, personal copies): fan documents kept out of the
  repository. Today: Nathan Williams' 2005 GameFAQs tech-tree guide (FAQ
  37448, redistribution forbidden), whose day-by-day opening validates the
  colony's turn loop; James Friel's 2005 GameFAQs planetary building guide
  (`gamefaqs-planets.txt`, credit required), every structure and project in
  words with the author's verdicts, and the mechanics of the ongoing projects
  (Endless Party and Scientist Takeover turn industry off and divert part of
  it, Automation frees a structure's workers, Terraforming turns black white).
  Its numbers disagree with planitem.txt (Agriplot prosperity 1 against the
  file's 2): the game's file wins. Nathan Williams' 1996/2005 ship and planet
  ballistics guide (`gamefaqs-gizmos-nathan-williams-2005.txt`, redistribution
  forbidden): measured weapon damage against each hull type and power unit,
  planetary shields and gizmos, for the combat model later.
- Other sources are read online: the fan manual at
  https://www.b-sting.nl/ascendancy/ (planet types, build power formulas,
  species, research list), and the game's own text files in the COB archives
  (planitem.txt, restree.txt, gizmos.txt, starstat.txt, help.txt...).
